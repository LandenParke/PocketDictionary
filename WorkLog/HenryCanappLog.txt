I used claude to debug until the display works.
Pin requirements:
VCC - 3.3v
GND - GND
CS - D10
RESET - D08
DC/RS - D09
SDI/MOSI(text is upside down) - D11
SCK - D13
LED - 3.3V (on extension board or whatever it's called)
SDO/MISO - D12
Make sure the voltage jumper is set to 3.3V (Thank you claude and Jake)


The following changes needed to be made to files for tests to compile.

On the actual test, use this constructor:
Ucglib_ILI9341_18x240x320_SWSPI ucg(/*sclk=*/ 13, /*data=*/ 11, /*cd=*/ 9, /*cs=*/ 10, /*reset=*/ 8);

In Arduino\libraries\Ucglib\src\Ucglib.cpp:
Add to top of file: 
#ifndef __NOP
#define __NOP __asm__ __volatile__("nop")
#endif

Change: volatile uint32_t *u8g_data_port[9];
to
volatile uint8_t *u8g_data_port[9];
