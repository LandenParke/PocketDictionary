Pin requirements:
VCC - 3.3v
GND - GND
CS - D10
RESET - D08
DC/RS - D09
SDI/MOSI(text is upside down) - D11
SCK - D13
LED - 3.3V (on extension board or whatever it's called)
SDO/MISO - D12
Make sure the voltage jumper is set to 3.3V (Thank you claude and Jake)


The following changes needed to be made to files for tests to compile.

Ucglib_ILI9341_18x240x320_SWSPI ucg(/*sclk=*/ 13, /*data=*/ 11, /*cd=*/ 9, /*cs=*/ 10, /*reset=*/ 8);
